Biography

What I work on

I direct the Directorate of Linkages and International Partnerships at Sa'adu Zungur University, where I build the international collaborations and research networks the university runs on. I previously served three terms as Head of the Department of Anatomy and as Unit Head of Human Genetics Informatics there.

Alongside that I am Senior Technical Officer for Pathogen Genomics at the Institute of Genomics, Africa CDC, supporting and coordinating genomic surveillance programmes at national public health institutes and regional sequencing hubs across West Africa. That work feeds directly into policy on molecular diagnostics, sequencing and outbreak response — the point is not the sequence data but the decisions it enables. I am also a visiting Bioinformatics Scientist at the Molecular Genetics and Infectious Disease (MOGID) Research Laboratory, Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University, and a certified Carpentries instructor.

My research uses high-throughput sequencing — bulk and single-cell RNA-Seq, whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing — to get at questions in cancer biology and infectious disease. In 2022 I founded Open Science Community Nigeria, and I serve on the Bioconductor Community Advisory Board and the board of INOSC. I founded BioSeq to provide bioinformatics services, analysis and hands-on training to researchers and institutions.
